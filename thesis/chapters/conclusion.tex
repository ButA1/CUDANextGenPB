\section{Linear Solver}
\label{sec:conclusion-linear-solver}

On a system with a single GPU, AMGX has presented itself as the perfect fix for NGPB. It outperformed LIS especially at larger problem sizes, yielding up to a 10x performance uplift.
I found that my (sub-)gather approach was much more memory efficient that the methods described in the admittedly very outdated AMGX Reference.
It allowed larger molecules like 6VYB to fit in the 10GB of VRAM of an RTX 3080.
However, this mechanism is done on the host side, and in case of a slow CPU, may result in a higher overall time for the linear solver stage.
I scaled well with better hardware, and even across multiple GPUs on a single cluster node.
When it came to two nodes however, the communication overhead either negated any performance benefits or even degraded performance.
This depended on the choice of preconditioner, as the performance degradation occurred with the AMG preconditioning.
